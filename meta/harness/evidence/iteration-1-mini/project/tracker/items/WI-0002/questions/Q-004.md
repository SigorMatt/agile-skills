## Context

Escalated because the answer depends on **intent no document records**, and because it becomes
**irreversible** the moment the first expense is stored (two of the four conditions in
`spec/question.md` §4).

`item.md` `## Notes` carries this as the one thing on this item to confirm rather than assume,
and it has been sitting there since your answers to Q-001..Q-003 were propagated. It is the last
thing standing between this item and a design, so it is being put to you directly.

When you answered Q-002 you said:

> *"If I paid and it's shared by all of us, include me automatically — most of the time I'm one
> of the people splitting it too."*

That sentence is conditional on *"if … it's shared by all of us"*, and the tool now handles that
case exactly as you asked: record an expense and name no sharers at all, and it is split between
everyone in the group including you, with nothing extra to type.

What your answer does **not** say is what should happen in the other case — when you *do* type a
list of sharers and your own name is not in it. `ADR-0003` decision 1 currently reads that list
literally: the sharers are exactly the people named, so leaving yourself off means you paid and
did not share, and you are owed the whole amount. `ADR-0003` decision 4 adds a note on stderr
when that happens, so it is at least visible.

Why it cannot wait for implementation: once expenses are stored, the data cannot distinguish
*"Alice deliberately did not share"* from *"Alice forgot to type her own name"*. Changing the
rule later would silently re-divide expenses already in the ledger, or leave them wrong forever.

## Question

When you record an expense, name the sharers explicitly, and do **not** include yourself in that
list — should the tool take you at your word and record that you paid but did not share, or
should it add you to the sharers anyway because you paid?

## Options considered

- **A — The list means literally what it says; you are not a sharer.** A note on stderr says so
  and the expense is still recorded, exit zero (`ADR-0003` decisions 1 and 4, and WI-0002 AC8 as
  currently written).
  Consequence: you can record "I paid for Bob and Carol's taxi, I wasn't in it", which is a real
  thing that happens. The risk is the opposite mistake: type `--with bob --with carol` when you
  meant "the three of us", and you are owed 100% instead of 67% — caught only if you read the
  stderr note.
- **B — You are always one of the sharers, whatever list you give.** Your name is added if it is
  missing.
  Consequence: the forgetful case is impossible to get wrong. But "I paid for someone else's
  thing and was not part of it" becomes impossible to express at all, and you would have to
  record it as something else or not at all.
- **C — Neither: refuse the command and make you say which you meant.** An explicit list that
  omits the payer is rejected with a message asking you to either include yourself or pass a flag
  confirming you did not share.
  Consequence: no silent wrong answer in either direction, at the cost of an error the first
  time you legitimately pay for other people.
- **Recommendation: A**, which is what `ADR-0003` already decided and what the criteria are
  written against — but the recommendation is weak and that is why you are being asked. It is
  chosen because it is the only option that can express both situations, and its failure mode is
  mitigated by the stderr note rather than eliminated. If you rarely or never pay for things you
  are not part of, **B** is genuinely better for you and we should change the ADR now rather
  than after there is data.
