## Question

When you want a month other than the current one, how do you want to name it —
`envel summary 2026-08`, or `envel summary --month 2026-08`?

## Options considered

- **A — A plain word: `envel summary 2026-08`.** `envel summary` on its own still gives the
  current month. Consequence: the shortest thing to type, and consistent with every other
  argument in the tool. The cost is that `summary` then takes a bare argument whose meaning you
  have to know — there is nothing on the line saying `2026-08` is a month rather than, say, an
  envelope name — and if we ever add a second optional thing to this command it cannot also be a
  plain word without the two becoming ambiguous.
- **B — A named option: `envel summary --month 2026-08`.** Consequence: the line says what the
  value is, it matches the choice you already made for a date on `envel spend`, and the command
  stays open to a second option later without anything becoming ambiguous. The cost is eight more
  characters every time you look at a past month, which by your own description is most times you
  run it.
- **C — Both accepted.** `envel summary 2026-08` and `envel summary --month 2026-08` do the same
  thing. Consequence: you never have to remember which one it is. The cost is that a tool with
  two ways to say one thing is a tool whose behaviour has to be explained twice, and giving both
  at once needs a rule nobody would guess.
- **Recommendation:** ours would be **A**, and we hold it weakly. Every other argument in this
  tool is a plain word, so A is the one that needs no explanation; and on your own account of how
  you use this — a month a couple of days after it has ended, checked against a statement — the
  month is the common case rather than the rare one, which is the side of your `WI-0002/Q-003`
  rule that points at the cheap form. This is our reading of your habits and not something you
  have told us: if you would rather the line said `--month` out loud, B costs us one line of code
  and we will build it.

## Answer

[human] A — `envel summary 2026-08`. Your reading of me is right: naming a month is the normal
case, not the rare one, so it gets the short form. And I'd rather everything in this tool be typed
the same way than have one command that's special.
