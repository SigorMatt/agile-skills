## Context

`WI-0001`, round 1, question 3 of 4: whether zero and negative amounts are allowed when putting
income in.

This is separate from question 2, which is about how a number is written. This one is about
which numbers mean something. `WI-0001` AC2 says income can be added; it does not say what
happens if the amount is `0` or `-40`. A negative "income" is really a correction — taking
money back out of an envelope — and whether you want that route is a decision about what the
tool is for, especially since correcting mistakes is currently out of scope and asked about at
`EP-001/Q-005`.

## Question

When putting income into an envelope, should an amount of zero, or a negative amount, be
accepted?

## Options considered

- **A — Both refused; the amount must be greater than zero.** Consequence: "put income in" only
  ever means more money, the listing can only go up from this command, and a mistyped minus sign
  is caught. The cost is that fixing an over-funded envelope needs whatever comes out of
  `EP-001/Q-005`.
- **B — Zero refused, negative allowed.** A negative amount takes money back out of the
  envelope. Consequence: you get a correction route immediately, without waiting for a decision
  on `EP-001/Q-005`; the cost is that the tool has two ways to reduce an envelope — this and
  recording a spend — and a month's summary has to decide which one a negative counts as.
- **C — Both allowed.** Consequence: nothing is ever refused; the cost is that a zero entry is
  recorded, shows up in the month's summary, and means nothing.
- **Recommendation:** ours would be A. It keeps "income" meaning one thing, and it leaves the
  correction route to be designed as a correction rather than arriving by accident as a negative
  income.

## Answer

[human] A — refuse both. Putting income in should only ever mean more money going in. I have asked for proper corrections and for moving money between envelopes in the epic's Q-005, so there is no need for a back door through a negative income.
