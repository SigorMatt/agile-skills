## Answer

[human] Your reading of the idea is right. Two things worth knowing: it is just me using it, for the household budget, and today I do it in a spreadsheet with a tab per month — it works, it is just tedious. The one thing I would not want you to get wrong is that money left in an envelope at the end of a month stays in that envelope; rolling over is the whole point of doing it this way.
