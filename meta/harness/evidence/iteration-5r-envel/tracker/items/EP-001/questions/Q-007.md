## Answer

**A — repair all of them in place as `provenance` corrections, under the policy settled at
`WI-0005/Q-009` and `BUG-0001/Q-002`.** Answered from the record: that policy is the recorded
decision for this exact shape, twice over, and it rests on `spec/doc-header.md` §4b, whose two
conditions both hold here — every assertion is unchanged, and every replacement citation resolves
and was read against the code before it was written.

The four the question asked about:

- `docs/architecture/overview.md` `## The parts` — `[src: envel/cli.py:68]` → `[src: envel/cli.py:154]`,
  which is `path = store.store_path()`. The two statements beside it, `document = store.load(path)`
  at `:156` and `store.save(path, result.store)` at `:218`, are named in the change-log row.
- `ADR-0007` `## Context` — `[src: envel/envelopes.py:50]` → `[src: envel/envelopes.py:63]`, which is
  `def balance(store, name):`.
- `ADR-0008` `## Decision` 3 and `## Decision` 4 — both `[src: envel/envelopes.py:50]` →
  `[src: envel/envelopes.py:63]`.

**And a fifth, which this question did not ask about.** `EP-001`'s review recorded `ADR-0010`'s
`[src: envel/envelopes.py:92]` as holding *with a mark* — it lands on `add_income`'s docstring while
the entry dict the sentence describes is thirteen lines below — and left the choice to this pass.
It is repaired too, to `[src: envel/envelopes.py:105]`. Deciding it the other way would leave one
citation of the five untreated for a reason no later reader could reconstruct without re-reading
the review; treating them alike costs one row in a document that was being versioned anyway.

**B was rejected** and it is the option worth saying something about. Citing a function name rather
than a line number would stop this class of defect recurring, and this is its third occurrence in
one engagement. But the citation grammar is `spec/doc-header.md` §4a's, not this project's, and
whether a looser form resolves is `scripts/lint-claims`' to decide rather than mine to assert. The
right home for it is a toolkit finding at `retro`, which `EP-001`'s review has already written down.
**C was rejected** because banking a claim that has been read and found true is F-067 exactly, and a
sanctioned repair exists. **D** would supersede two ADRs whose decisions are both still correct.
