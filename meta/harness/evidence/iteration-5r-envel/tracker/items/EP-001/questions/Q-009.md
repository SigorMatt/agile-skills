## Context

You asked for a command-line tool for envelope budgeting: *"I set up named envelopes, put income
into them, record spending against an envelope, and see what's left in each. I also want a simple
monthly summary I can look at. Data must survive between runs. Python, no services."*

Later you told us what you were replacing — a spreadsheet with a tab per month, which works and is
tedious — and the one rule you were most worried we would get wrong: **money left in an envelope at
the end of a month stays in that envelope** [src: EP-001/Q-001]. You asked for two more things once
you saw the shape of it: moving money between envelopes, for the week an envelope runs short, and
correcting a spend you had already recorded, because you mistype things most weeks
[src: EP-001/Q-005].

All of that is built. Nothing is left in flight, nothing is blocked, and no question anywhere is
waiting on anybody. So this is the point at which we stop and ask you whether what is there is what
you wanted. It is the only question in this round and the last thing the engagement needs from you.

Here is what the tool does now, run end to end from an empty store, one command per line, quitting
between every one:

```
$ envel new groceries                              created envelope groceries
$ envel add groceries 400                          added 400.00 to groceries, which now holds 400.00
$ envel spend groceries 42.30 "co-op"              spent 42.30 from groceries, which now holds 357.70
$ envel new fun                                    created envelope fun
$ envel move groceries fun 30                      moved 30.00 from groceries, which now holds 327.70,
                                                   to fun, which now holds 30.00
$ envel list                                       fun  30.00
                                                   groceries  327.70
$ envel summary 2026-09                            fun  in 0.00  spent 0.00  moved 30.00  left 30.00
                                                   groceries  in 400.00  spent 42.30  moved -30.00  left 327.70
$ envel spend groceries 9999                       groceries holds 400.00, which is less than 9999.00.
                                                   Nothing has been recorded; move money into it first
                                                   if you want to spend that.
$ envel fix 2 --amount 45.00                       corrected entry 2 in groceries, which now holds 75.00
$ envel entries groceries --month 2026-08          groceries held 0.00 at the start of 2026-08
                                                   5  2026-08-15  spend  groceries  -250.00  august shop
                                                   groceries was 250.00 short at the end of 2026-08
```

## Question

Seven pieces of work were opened under this epic. Every one of them is closed and delivered, and
here is each by name so that nothing reaches you only because we remembered it:

- **`WI-0001` — set up envelopes, put income into them, and see what is in each. Delivered.**
  `envel new`, `envel add` and `envel list`, with amounts written to two decimal places and
  nothing rounded, and envelope names matched whatever their capitalisation.
- **`WI-0002` — record spending against an envelope and see what is left. Delivered.**
  `envel spend`, dated today unless you write `--on`, with an optional description, and an
  overspend refused with what the envelope actually holds.
- **`WI-0003` — show a summary of a month. Delivered.** `envel summary 2026-08`, one row per
  envelope that existed by the end of that month, four figures: in, spent, moved, left.
- **`WI-0004` — move money between envelopes. Delivered.** `envel move groceries fun 30`, which
  is the thing you said you would need the week an envelope runs short.
- **`WI-0005` — correct a spend that was already recorded. Delivered.** `envel fix <ref>` for the
  amount, date, description or envelope, and `envel remove <ref>` for an entry that should not be
  there. It reaches an income as well as a spend.
- **`WI-0006` — look up what has been recorded against an envelope. Delivered.**
  `envel entries [envelope] [--month YYYY-MM]`, which is where you read off the short number you
  type back into `fix` or `remove`. This is the one item you did not ask for: we derived it from a
  phrase of yours, offered you the chance to close it, and you kept it [src: WI-0006/Q-001].
- **`BUG-0001` — a past month's closing figure could show a negative amount. Delivered.** We found
  this ourselves, against your own rule that no envelope should ever go negative, and you chose what
  to do about it: the month says how short it closed, in words [src: BUG-0001/Q-001].

**What we decided on your behalf, and under which of your answers.** You gave us one standing
licence, and this is where you see what it bought before you accept anything:

- **`WI-0003/Q-004`** — *"I'd rather everything in this tool be typed the same way than have one
  command that's special."* We read that as covering **argument style and the naming of commands
  and options**, and spent it twice:
  - **`envel fix <ref> --amount --on --description --envelope` and `envel remove <ref>`**
    (`WI-0005`). The reference is a plain word because the command always takes one; each field
    you might change is a named option because you rarely change more than one. We also chose the
    word `remove` rather than `unspend`.
  - **`envel entries [<envelope>] [--month <YYYY-MM>]`** (`WI-0006`). The envelope is a plain
    word; the month is `--month` even though `envel summary` takes its month as a plain word,
    because for `entries` you asked for *this month unless I name one* [src: WI-0006/Q-002] and so
    the month is the rare argument. We rejected `envel entries groceries 2026-08` because an
    envelope may legally be called `2026-08`, and a rule reading a bare word as a month would make
    that envelope unreachable — a rule about your data we were not willing to invent.

**And one piece of printed wording you have never seen.** You approved
*"groceries was 250.00 short at the end of 2026-08"* in your own words at `BUG-0001/Q-001`. Two
sentences beside it were ours, taken under **no** licence: the summary row's last column reads
`short 250.00` instead of `left -250.00`, and a listing that opens on a short month reads
*"groceries was 250.00 short at the **start** of 2026-09"*. If either is wrong, say so — it is a
wording change and nothing else.

**Do you accept this engagement as it stands?**
