## Context

This is the only question on `BUG-0001`, and the only one being asked this round.

While checking the whole engagement against your success measures before asking you to accept
it, we ran the tool against your rule *"No negative envelopes anywhere in this tool"* and found
one place where it is not kept. The tool never lets an envelope's **current** balance go below
zero — a spend bigger than what is left is refused, and so is a correction or a removal that
would do the same. But a **past month's closing figure** can be negative. Both of these are real
output from the tool as it stands:

```
$ envel entries groceries --month 2026-08
groceries held 0.00 at the start of 2026-08
2  2026-08-14  spend  groceries  -250.00  august shop
groceries holds -250.00 at the end of 2026-08

$ envel summary 2026-08
groceries  in 0.00  spent 250.00  moved 0.00  left -250.00
```

Here is how it arises, because it matters for choosing between the options. Two of your earlier
decisions meet in an awkward place:

- a **spend** counts in the month it happened, because you asked to be able to date one:
  *"a Saturday shop landing in the wrong month would make the monthly summary wrong"*
  [src: WI-0002/Q-001];
- **income** counts in the month you typed it, and cannot be dated, because you said
  *"I put income in when it arrives — that's the one thing I'm never late on"*
  [src: WI-0003/Q-003], and you declined again when we asked whether a correction could put a
  date on an income [src: WI-0005/Q-006].

So when you sit down *"a couple of days after the month has ended with the statement in front of
me"* [src: WI-0005/Q-001] and type in last month's shopping, the spends land in last month while
the money that covers them sits in this month. If last month's own income does not cover what you
date into it, last month closes short — and the tool prints that shortfall as a minus number. Your
current balance is still right, and no money is lost or double-counted.

We are asking rather than choosing because every way out of this changes something **you** have
already decided, and none of them is ours to reverse. `EP-001` cannot be signed off until this is
settled: *"No envelope ever shows a negative amount"* is one of its success measures.

## Question

When a past month's closing figure for an envelope would be negative — because spends dated into
that month are more than the money recorded in it — what should the tool do?

## Options considered

- **A — Nothing. Treat the rule as being about the current balance.** No code changes. We would
  record that the rule covers what an envelope holds now, amend `EP-001`'s success measure to say
  so in those words, and close this bug as dropped with your answer as the reason. Consequence:
  your way of working is untouched — you keep typing last month's spends whenever you sit down
  with the statement, and every figure keeps adding up. The cost is that `envel summary` and
  `envel entries` will go on printing a minus figure for a past month, which is the thing you
  twice said you did not want to see.

- **B — Refuse the spend that would do it.** `envel spend groceries 250.00 --on 2026-08-14` would
  be refused when August's own money does not cover it, the way an overspend is refused today.
  Consequence: a minus number becomes impossible everywhere, by the same rule you already have.
  The cost is the serious one: there is no way to put money **into** August once August is over,
  because income cannot be dated. So the tool would refuse a spend that really happened and offer
  you no way to record it — your only move would be to date it into this month instead, which is
  exactly the *"Saturday shop landing in the wrong month"* you asked us to prevent.

- **C — Keep recording it, and say it in words instead of a minus.** The spend is recorded as
  now; the two lines change so that no minus number appears — for example
  `groceries was 250.00 short at the end of 2026-08` in place of `holds -250.00`, and the
  summary's last column showing the same thing. Consequence: your rule is kept, your way of
  working is untouched, and nothing is refused. The cost is ours to carry and is worth naming:
  those lines are the ones you asked to *add up* — *"a list I cannot add up costs me an evening"*
  [src: WI-0006/Q-004] — and a figure in words no longer adds up as a number, so two criteria you
  have already accepted (`WI-0003` AC3 and `WI-0006` AC9) would have to be amended by a follow-up
  item before this could be built.

- **D — Let income be dated, the way a spend is.** `envel add groceries 500.00 --on 2026-08-01`,
  so August's money can be put into August and the month closes level. Consequence: the problem
  disappears at its root rather than being handled, and late reconciliation of a whole month
  becomes possible. The cost is that this reverses a decision you have taken twice
  [src: WI-0003/Q-003] [src: WI-0005/Q-006], it makes `WI-0003` AC8 false, and it is the largest
  of the four changes — it reaches the stored file, the summary and the correction command.

- **Recommendation:** ours is **C**, and we are not confident enough in it to want you to take it
  on our say-so. It is the only option that keeps both of the things you have told us you want —
  no minus number, and freedom to type last month's spends late — and its whole cost falls on us
  rather than on you. We would rank **A** second, because it is honest and costs nothing, if on
  reading this you find that what you meant was *"don't show me an envelope I have overdrawn
  today"*. We would avoid **B**: it enforces your rule by refusing to record something that
  actually happened, with no way for you to make it acceptable. **D** we mention because it is
  the real root of it, not because we think you have changed your mind.

## Answer

[human] **C.** I said no minus numbers without qualification and I meant it — I am not going to
water that down into "no minus numbers except in the places it was awkward" to save the work, so A
is out. "Groceries was 250.00 short at the end of 2026-08" tells me exactly what I need and still
gives me the figure, and a figure I can read is what the adding-up rule was about; a minus sign in
front of it is what I don't want. B refuses to record a shop that actually happened, which is no
use to me, and D is dating income, which I have now turned down twice and am not revisiting.
