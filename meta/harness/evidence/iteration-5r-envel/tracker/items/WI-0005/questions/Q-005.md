## Context

`WI-0005`, round 1, question 5 of 5, and the last of them — that is all we need from you on this
item for now.

This one is here because two of your own answers point in different directions and we are not
willing to choose between them for you.

At `EP-001/Q-005` you asked to be able to correct **a spend**: *"being able to correct a spend I
already recorded matters to me more than keeping a perfect history of my own typos."* We took
that word literally and this item is scoped to spends.

But at `WI-0001/Q-003` you refused a zero or negative income, and the reason you gave was this:
*"Putting income in should only ever mean more money going in. I have asked for proper
corrections and for moving money between envelopes in the epic's Q-005, so there is no need for a
back door through a negative income."* You turned down one way of fixing a mistyped income **on
the grounds that corrections were coming**. If corrections only ever reach spends, then that
reasoning does not hold up: a payslip you typed as 1800.00 instead of 180.00 would have no way
back at all, and the negative income you refused was the only route.

There is one more thing you have said that bears on how far to take this. At `WI-0004/Q-001`,
choosing whether a move could be dated, you wrote: *"The thing that settles it for me is that I
can fix a spend afterwards and I can't fix a move, so I need to be able to get it right when I
type it."* You have already decided a move is not fixable, and `WI-0004` was built on that
sentence — so we are not offering to reopen it lightly.

## Question

Should you be able to correct an income you have already put into an envelope, in the same way
as a spend?

## Options considered

- **A — Spends only, this round.** Consequence: the item stays exactly the size you asked for,
  and it is the smaller thing to build. The cost is the one above: a mistyped income has no
  route back, and the reason you gave for refusing a negative income assumed there would be one.
  You would be choosing to live with a wrong income figure until it becomes worth its own item.
- **B — Spends and income.** Consequence: everything you type in by hand can be put right, which
  is what your `WI-0001/Q-003` reasoning assumed. The cost is roughly half as much work again on
  this item, and correcting an income downward can take an envelope negative in the same way
  question 4 of this round describes — so whatever you answer there would have to apply here too.
- **C — Spends, income and moves.** Consequence: nothing typed into this tool is permanent.
  The cost is that it contradicts the sentence `WI-0004` was designed around — *"I can fix a
  spend afterwards and I can't fix a move"* — and `WI-0004` is built and closed, so this would
  be new work against a delivered item rather than a change to this one. You were also clear at
  `WI-0003/Q-003` that you are *"not paying for rework on a command that already works for a case
  I don't have"*.
- **D — Spends now; income as its own item, prioritised separately.** Consequence: this item
  stays the size you asked for and ships sooner, and the income gap is on the board where you can
  see it rather than being forgotten. The cost is that the two corrections would be designed
  twice and would probably end up spelled slightly differently, which is the sort of
  inconsistency you objected to at `WI-0003/Q-004`.
- **Recommendation:** ours would be **B**, and the reason is your own sentence at
  `WI-0001/Q-003` rather than anything we think: you refused the negative-income back door
  *because* corrections were coming, and A leaves that trade without the thing it was traded
  for. We are not recommending **C** — you have settled that a move is not fixable and we would
  rather take you at your word. If income really is the one thing you never get wrong, **A** is a
  fine answer and we will record it as your decision rather than as an oversight.

## Answer

[human] B — spends and income. I turned down the negative income on the understanding that proper
corrections were coming, so corrections had better reach an income; a payslip typed with the
wrong number of noughts is worse than a mistyped shop. Not moves — that stays as I decided it.
