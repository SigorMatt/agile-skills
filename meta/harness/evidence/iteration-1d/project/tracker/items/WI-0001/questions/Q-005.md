## Context

AC1 says adding a person whose name is already recorded is refused rather than creating a
duplicate. AC3 says the payer and every sharer must already be a recorded person. Both turn on
what "already recorded" means, and the item does not say.

If you have added `Ana` and you then type `ana`, there are only two possible behaviours and they
are opposites: either the tool refuses the duplicate, or you now have two people in your group who
are the same person, and the who-owes-whom report will forever show them separately. There is no
third answer, and `verify` cannot ask anyone which one you meant.

This is the smallest question in this batch, and I am asking it rather than assuming because it
is a behaviour you will hit within the first five minutes of using the tool.

## Question

Are two names that differ only in capitalisation or surrounding spaces — `Ana`, `ana`, ` Ana ` —
the same person, or different people?

## Options considered

- **A — the same person.** Names are compared with surrounding spaces trimmed and case ignored, so
  `ana` is refused as a duplicate of `Ana`, and `pay ana` finds Ana. The name is stored and
  displayed as you first typed it. Consequence: matches how people actually type; the only cost is
  that you cannot have both `ana` and `Ana` as two genuinely different people, which is not a real
  scenario in a friend group.
- **B — different people.** Comparison is exact. Consequence: trivially simple and completely
  literal; one stray capital letter silently creates a second Ana who owes money separately, and
  since this item has no delete, you would be stuck with her.
- **Recommendation:** **A.** B's failure mode is silent, permanent in an item with no delete, and
  produces a wrong answer to the one question the tool exists to answer. A one-word "yes, A" is
  enough here.

## Answer

[human] Same person — A.

**Recorded by `answer-questions`:** this selects **option A** — names are compared with
surrounding whitespace trimmed and case ignored, and stored and displayed in the form first
typed.
