## Context

WI-0001 is the item that records people and expenses. I am refining it against the Definition of
Ready, and criterion R4 says every acceptance criterion must be decidable by someone with a
terminal and no context. Two of its criteria are not, and this question is the larger of the two
reasons.

AC3 currently says an expense is recorded with "a payer, an amount, a description, and the set of
people who shared it". It does not say **how much each sharer is on the hook for.** `intake`
recorded this as an unknown on purpose rather than guessing it (`WI-0001/item.md`, `## Notes`,
first bullet).

Your idea said "shared by some or all", which settles *who* shared an expense but not *in what
proportion*. Both readings are ordinary in a tool like this, and they are not a small difference:
they change what you type at the command line, what the store holds, and the arithmetic in
WI-0002's who-owes-whom report.

## Question

When an expense is shared, is it always split **equally** among the people who shared it, or do
you need to be able to say that some people owe more of it than others?

## Options considered

- **A — equal splits only.** A €60 dinner shared by three people is €20 each, always. Consequence:
  the simplest thing to type (you name who shared it and nothing else) and the simplest report.
  If your group ever has a case where one person had the expensive thing, you would have to fake
  it by splitting the bill into two recorded expenses.
- **B — equal by default, uneven when you say so.** You normally just name the sharers and get an
  equal split; when you need to, you state each person's amount or share explicitly, and the tool
  checks the parts add up to the total. Consequence: covers both cases and is still one command.
  Costs a little more design in WI-0001 and one more rule in WI-0002.
- **C — always state every person's amount.** Most precise, and the most typing for the common
  case, which is the thing you said you wanted less of.
- **Recommendation:** **A**, unless you know of a real case in your group that needs B. Your stated
  goal is to stop typing expenses in, and A is the least typing; splitting an odd bill into two
  recorded expenses is a workable escape hatch and costs nothing to build. I would rather ship A
  and add uneven shares later as its own item than build B on a guess that you need it. If you
  say B, I will record it here and it becomes part of this item.

## Answer

[human] Equal split's fine for now. If we hit a case that needs otherwise we'll deal with it then.

**Recorded by `answer-questions`:** this selects **option A** — equal splits only, with no
per-person share ever entered or stored. "We'll deal with it then" is why uneven splits are
recorded in `item.md` under `## Out of scope` and in the vision as a stated product boundary,
rather than simply left unmentioned: the next person to want them should find the decision, not
a silence.
