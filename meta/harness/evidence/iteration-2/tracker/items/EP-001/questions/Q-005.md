## Context

Rather than describe it, here is the tool doing the five things you said it was for, run just now

**It shows its work before it does anything.** The folder was byte-for-byte unchanged afterwards:

```

recent/documents/notes.txt  recent/documents/report.pdf  recent/documents/report_dup.pdf
```

One thing you should know before you answer, because it was your decision and it is easy to
forget: **`tidy` does not go into subfolders.** Pointed at a folder it tidies the files sitting
directly in it, and folders already there are neither entered nor moved. You settled that during
intake [src: EP-001/Q-003]. If you have since changed your mind, that is a fourth work item and
this is the moment to say so.
