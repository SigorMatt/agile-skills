## Question

When a roll is shown to you, what does the breakdown have to contain for the total to be
believable?

## Options considered

Examples use `3d6+2`, rolling 4, 5 and 2, for a total of 13. The exact spacing and punctuation is
not what is being asked about — that is ours to settle — only what information is present.

- **A — every die's face, and the modifier, shown separately.** `3d6+2 → 13  [4, 5, 2] +2`.
  Consequence: the smallest thing from which you can re-add the total by hand. Every roll prints
  in the same shape, including a bare `d20`, which makes a column of them easy to skim.
- **B — A, plus the dice subtotal before the modifier.** `3d6+2 → 13  [4, 5, 2] = 11, +2`.
  Consequence: one extra number per line. It saves you adding up the dice yourself, which starts
  to matter around six or eight dice and is pure noise at two.
- **C — A, but only when there is something to break down.** A roll with several dice or a
  modifier prints as in A; a bare `d20` prints just its number. Consequence: the least noise for
  the commonest single-die roll, at the cost of the output not having the same shape every time —
  which makes the history listing in `WI-0002` ragged and makes "did it show me the breakdown?"
  a question with two answers.
- **Recommendation:** A. It is one shape for every roll, it is the least we can show and still
  have you believe the number, and it is the reading of your own word "breakdown" that adds
  nothing you did not ask for. B is worth having if you routinely roll handfuls of dice, and C is
  right if `d20` is most of what you type — we cannot tell which from here, so it is yours.

## Answer

Option **A** — every die's face and the modifier shown separately — in plain text. The
stakeholder's words:

> [human] A — the total plus the individual dice, plain text. That's all I need to believe the number.

They named the option by its letter, so option A's content is what stands: the total, each die's
face individually, and the modifier separately, in one shape for every roll — a bare `d20` prints
in the same form as `3d6+2` rather than printing its number alone. That is the difference between
A and C, and A is what they chose.

Their own gloss — *"the total plus the individual dice"* — does not repeat the modifier. It is
read as shorthand rather than as a narrowing, for two reasons that are worth stating because the
reading is ours: the option they selected shows the modifier, and dropping it would make `3d6+2`
un-re-addable, which is the opposite of *"all I need to believe the number"*. The epic's success
measure said the same thing before they answered, as intake's guess; it is now theirs
[src: EP-001].

*"Plain text"* is new and is kept: the breakdown is characters on a terminal, not a table, colour
or any other rendering. What is still **not** settled by this answer is the layout — spacing,
brackets, where the total sits — and the question said so when it was asked. That stays `plan`'s,
by the same test that routed the error stream there, and it is recorded in the item's `## Notes`
rather than decided here.
