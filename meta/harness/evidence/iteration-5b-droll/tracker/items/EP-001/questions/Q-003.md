## Answer

Nothing was contradicted, and two of the five assumptions the question listed were confirmed. The
stakeholder's words:

> [human] It's really just for me and my gaming group on game night — nothing bigger than that. And keeping the history for just the current session is fine; being able to save it across sessions would be nice someday but I don't need it now.

Three things are taken from this and routed:

1. **The audience and the setting** — themselves and their gaming group on game night, *"nothing
   bigger than that"*. This confirms the assumption that nothing is shared or networked and adds
   no requirement of its own; it is recorded where an exclusion can be checked against it rather
   than argued from memory.
2. **Session-only history, confirmed.** *"Keeping the history for just the current session is
   fine"* is the assumption the question stated, in their own words, and it is now `WI-0002`'s
   AC5 rather than an assumption.
3. **Cross-session saving is a want for later, and no item is filed for it.** *"Would be nice
   someday but I don't need it now"* declines the work rather than asking for it, so filing a
   `draft` item under this epic would put on the board something they said they do not want yet.
   It is recorded in `EP-001`'s `## Out of scope` and in the vision, in their words, which is
   where a later `tracker/requests/` entry would be checked against. If that reading is wrong,
   the correction costs one item.

The three assumptions the question listed that they did not mention — a text interface, no
game-system knowledge, and no seeded or reproducible randomness — stay assumptions, and the
epic's `## Out of scope` now says so of itself rather than pointing at this question as an
opportunity that is still open.
