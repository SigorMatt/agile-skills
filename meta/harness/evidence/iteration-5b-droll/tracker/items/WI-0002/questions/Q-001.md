## Question

When you type `history` and droll lists this session's rolls back to you, what should each entry
show?

## Options considered

Examples show a session in which you rolled `3d6+2` (dice 4, 5, 2) and then `d20` (a 17).

- **A — the expression and its total, and nothing else.**

  ```
  3d6+2 -> 13
  d20 -> 17
  ```

  Consequence: the shortest list, and the easiest to skim when there are thirty of them on a
  game night. You can see what you rolled and what it came to. You cannot see which dice made a
  total, so a surprising old number is something you have to take on trust or roll again.

- **B — the same line the roll printed at the time, breakdown and all.**

  ```
  3d6+2 -> 13   [4, 5, 2] +2
  d20 -> 17   [17]
  ```

  Consequence: the list is a transcript — what you saw then is what you see now, in one shape for
  every entry. A ten-minute-old total is as checkable as a fresh one. The lines are longer, and a
  handful of dice makes them noticeably longer, so thirty entries is a wall of text rather than a
  column of numbers.

- **C — A by default, with the breakdown for one entry on request.** The list prints as in A, and
  a second input (say `history 4`) reprints entry 4 in full. Consequence: short list *and* the
  detail when you want it. It costs a second thing to remember how to type, it needs the entries
  numbered so you can name one, and it is more to build and more to get wrong — for a tool used
  by one gaming group, that may be machinery nobody uses.

- **Recommendation: B, and this is our preference rather than a finding.** Two things point at
  it. First, your own reason for the breakdown — *"all I need to believe the number"* — reads as
  being about believing the number, not about believing it within five seconds of rolling it, and
  the moment you are most likely to doubt a total is when someone questions it later. Second, you
  already chose one shape for every roll over a shape that varies: at `WI-0001/Q-001` you took
  option A over option C, and option C was the one that would have made this list ragged. B keeps
  that consistency. A is the right answer if what you actually want here is a scoreboard to skim,
  and we cannot tell which from where we are sitting — which is why it is yours.
