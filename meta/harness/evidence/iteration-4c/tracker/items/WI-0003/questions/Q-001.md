## Question

When you want to delete a card, how do you tell the tool which one?

## Options considered

- **A — By its front side, typed out.** `delete "bank"` removes the card whose front is exactly
  that. Consequence: nothing to look up and nothing to remember, and it reads like the card
  itself. You have to type the front exactly as you entered it, and if two cards share a front,
  the tool has to ask you which — or refuse.
- **B — By a number the tool gives each card.** Every card has a short number, and you delete by
  it. Consequence: never ambiguous, and it is short to type. It only works if you can *see* the
  numbers, which means the tool also needs a way to list your cards — that is real extra work
  which no item currently records, and we would file it as its own item rather than smuggle it in
  here.
- **C — Pick it during a review.** While a card is in front of you in a session, a key removes it.
  Consequence: the moment you most often notice a card is wrong is when it comes up, and this is
  the shortest path from noticing to gone. It only reaches cards that are due, so a card you want
  rid of might not be reachable for a month.
- **Recommendation (ours, not yours to defer to):** **A**, on its own for now. It needs nothing
  that does not already exist, and it matches how you described using the tool — a terminal, a
  card is its front side. B and C are both good and both are additional work; if you want either,
  say so and we will scope it as its own item rather than pretend it is free.

## Answer

[human] A — by typing the front side. I don't need a numbered list for this.
