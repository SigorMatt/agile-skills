## Answer

The stakeholder's reply, verbatim:

> Whatever you think is best on the order. But no — I don't want to drop either one, the
> import's part of what I asked for too.

Two things, and they are answered separately because they are different.

**The order is delegated, so `answer-questions` decides it: option A — WI-0001, then WI-0002,
then WI-0003.** The basis is the record rather than taste. WI-0002 is what makes the tool answer
the question the stakeholder built it for ("at any point show who owes whom", `IDEA.md`), so it
is the first item after WI-0001 that delivers the epic's stated outcome. WI-0003 cannot start at
all until the bank CSV sample arrives (Q-001), so sequencing it earlier would not make it happen
earlier. This is a delegation, not a preference of the stakeholder's, and the record says so.

**Neither item is dropped.** "The import's part of what I asked for too" is a statement that
WI-0003 is required for the epic's stated outcome, not merely wanted — which under
`spec/ids-and-statuses.md` §6 is `high`, not `medium`. WI-0003's priority is raised to `high`
accordingly. This does not disturb the order: the orchestrator's selection key breaks a
priority tie on `created` ascending, and WI-0002 was created two seconds before WI-0003, so
WI-0002 is still selected first. Raising the priority records that the epic is not coherent
without the importer; it does not reorder anything.

The consequence of not dropping WI-0003 is that this epic cannot end as fully delivered until
the sample arrives. WI-0003 is parked at `blocked` (Q-001), and when the engagement reaches rest
the termination question will name it as not delivered and ask the stakeholder what they want to
do about it. That is the honest path, and it is why WI-0003 is parked rather than quietly
dropped.
