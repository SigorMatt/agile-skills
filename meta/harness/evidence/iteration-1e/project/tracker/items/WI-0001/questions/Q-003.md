## Context

WI-0001, refinement round 1, question 3 of 3 — the last one in this batch, and that is all we
are asking about this item for now.

Intake put "editing or deleting a person or an expense once recorded" in this item's out-of-scope
list, and wrote alongside it that refinement should confirm with you whether it is actually
wanted. This is that confirmation.

As things stand, everything you record is permanent. Type 130 when you meant 13, or add "Cara"
when your friend spells it "Kara", and there is no way to fix it from the tool — the only remedy
is to edit the data file by hand, which the epic's own success measures say a person should never
have to do. Against that: the more of this tool that is only ever appended to, the simpler and
safer it is, and a correction command is real work that has to be specified and checked.

There is a related decision hiding in here that we have already taken, so you know: a person is
identified by their name exactly as typed, so "Ana" and "ana" are two different people. That is
the behaviour that never silently merges two friends into one — but it does mean a typo creates a
person, and whether you can get rid of that person is exactly what this question is about.

## Question

Do you want to be able to correct or remove a person or an expense you have already recorded —
and if so, should that be part of this first piece of work, or a separate one after the tool can
answer "who owes whom"?

## Options considered

- **A — No corrections. The record is append-only.** Consequence: the smallest, safest tool; a
  mistake is permanent and lives in every "who owes whom" answer until you edit the data file by
  hand. Fine if you are careful and the group is small.
- **B — Corrections in this item.** A way to remove or amend a person and an expense, built and
  verified as part of WI-0001. Consequence: the tool is usable by someone who makes mistakes,
  from the first version. It makes this first item noticeably bigger, and it delays WI-0002 —
  the "who owes whom" answer you said is the point of the tool — by however long it takes.
- **C — Corrections as their own work item, after WI-0002.** WI-0001 stays append-only for now,
  and a new item is filed under EP-001 for removing and amending records, scheduled after the
  settlement list lands. Consequence: you get the answer you built the tool for soonest, and the
  mistake-fixing arrives shortly after, specified properly instead of bolted on.
- **Recommendation:** C. Something will get typed wrong, so A is not really a long-term answer —
  but the thing you actually asked for is the who-owes-whom list, and putting corrections ahead
  of it would push that back for a problem you can work around for a couple of items. If you
  would rather not be able to make a mistake you cannot undo at all, say B and we will fold it
  into this item.

## Answer

The stakeholder's reply, verbatim:

> Hadn't really thought about it, but if I have to pick — being able to delete a mistake matters
> more to me than editing one. Timing's up to you, doesn't need to hold up the who-owes-whom
> feature.

Three separate things are settled here, and they are recorded separately because they are
different decisions.

**Deletion is wanted.** Option A — append-only for ever — is refused. A mistaken person or
expense must be removable from the tool, without hand-editing the data file, which EP-001's own
success measures already forbid.

**Editing is not.** They were offered correcting and removing together and picked removal when
made to choose. So editing in place is out of scope, and nothing is scheduled for it: a
correction is a deletion and a re-record. That is recorded as a decision of theirs, not as an
oversight, so that nobody later reads "delete" as shorthand for "delete and edit".

**The timing is delegated, with one constraint**, which makes it **option C** rather than option
B: it must not hold up the who-owes-whom feature. So deletion is not folded into WI-0001, and it
does not go before WI-0002.

This answer widens the scope beyond what any item records, so under `spec/ids-and-statuses.md` §5
`answer-questions` has filed the implied work rather than growing WI-0001 around it:

- **WI-0004 — "Delete a person or an expense recorded by mistake"**, at `draft` under EP-001,
  `arose-from: WI-0001/Q-003`, `depends-on: WI-0002` (the stakeholder's constraint made
  mechanical), priority `medium` — because `high` is reserved for what the epic's stated outcome
  requires, and EP-001's goal and success measures are coherent without deletion. Their own
  framing, *"if I have to pick"*, is a want rather than a requirement.

Its acceptance criteria are a first statement, not a refined one: WI-0004 is at `draft` and has
its own refinement ahead of it, where the command names and how a recorded expense is identified
on the command line have to be settled. WI-0001 itself is unchanged in scope and stays
append-only.
