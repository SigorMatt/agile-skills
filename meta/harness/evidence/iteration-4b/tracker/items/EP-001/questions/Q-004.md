## Context

You asked for this:

> "A flashcard tool: add cards, review due cards daily, simple spaced repetition. Progress
> persists."

and then, when we asked what would make it a failure, you told us two things:

> "don't lose my progress — that's the one thing that would make this a failure, along with a
> review session that drags on more than a couple minutes"

Everything we were asked to build has stopped moving. Every work item is closed, the bug we
found along the way is fixed and closed, no question anywhere is waiting on anyone, and there is
nothing left in the pipeline that can advance on its own. That is the moment this pipeline is
required to come back to you rather than declare itself finished, so here we are: this is the
one decision that is yours and not ours.

This is the only question in this round, and it is the last one on this engagement unless your
answer creates more.

**What you can do with it today.** There is one command, `recall`, with four subcommands — `add`,
`list`, `review`, `delete`. Your deck lives in a single file under your home directory
(`~/.local/share/recall/deck.json` unless you set `RECALL_DECK`), which survives the process
ending and a reboot. `docs/process/using-recall.md` walks through all four from scratch and is
written for someone who has never seen the source.

**The scheduling rule you chose** is the ladder `1, 3, 7, 30` days from `EP-001/Q-003`: a card
you recall correctly moves up one rung and then stays at 30 days; a card you get wrong drops
straight back to one day. A sitting tells you, per card, when you will next see it.

**The things we know are true and you may not.** None of these breaks a criterion you agreed to;
all are recorded in the items and in `docs/process/using-recall.md` `## What this version does
not do yet`:

- Editing a card is not possible — you drew that line yourself ("editing can wait"). Changing a
  card's wording means deleting it and adding it again, which restarts that card's schedule at
  the bottom of the ladder.
- Deleting is one card at a time, named by typing its question side **exactly** as `recall list`
  shows it. A stray trailing space gets you "no card has that question" rather than a guess.
- Nothing is remembered about past sittings — the deck holds how the *last* review of each card
  went and no more. No tallies, no streaks, no end-of-sitting summary.
- The four gaps in the ladder are fixed; there is no way to change them or to reschedule a card
  by hand.
- Your "couple of minutes" sitting is not a criterion anywhere. You chose that deliberately at
  `WI-0002/Q-001` — *"No limit — A. Show me everything that's due"* — so a sitting shows
  everything due, and how long it takes is up to how much you let build up. We designed towards
  it; nothing measures it.
