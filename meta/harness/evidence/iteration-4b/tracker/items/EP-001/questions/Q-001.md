The other two ask you to choose between options we have prepared. This one does not, and it is
deliberately the one we cannot write for you.

Everything we have asked so far comes from our list of things we needed to know in order to
split the work. What we have built from your sentence — "add cards, review due cards daily,
simple spaced repetition, progress persists" — is an epic, three work items, and a set of
exclusions we invented on your behalf, because you did not mention them. Those exclusions are in
`tracker/items/EP-001/item.md` under **Out of scope**, and they are the most likely place for
us to have quietly decided something you care about. In short, we assumed: one person on one
machine, text-only cards, one deck, no syncing, no import or export from other flashcard tools,
no statistics or streaks, and no editing or deleting a card once it is added.

This question is not blocking. Nothing waits on it. But an answer given now costs far less than
the same answer given at the end.

## Question

Anything counts: something in that exclusion list that should not be excluded, something about
how it should feel to use, a constraint on the machine or language, something you have used
before and liked or hated, or a requirement you have been assuming we already knew.
