## Question

amounts and the merchant names can be changed to anything you like — it is the shape that matters:
the column names, the date format, and whether money leaving your account is negative, positive, or
in a separate column from money coming in.

## Options considered

This is a missing fact rather than a choice between designs, so these are about what happens next
rather than about the format itself.

- **A — you paste the sample here** (or the path to a file on this machine). Consequence: WI-0004
  is refined against the real thing and built, and the epic closes. This is the only option that
  ends with an importer that works on your actual statement.
- **B — you name your bank and we work from its published export format.** Consequence: work can
  start immediately, and every criterion then rests on a documented guess that must be re-checked
  against a real export before the item can be called done. You would end up doing the pasting
  anyway, after more had been built on top of it.
- **C — one row is enough.** If pasting three rows is the friction, paste the header line and a
  single transaction. Consequence: enough to build against; the "money coming in" case would be
  assumed rather than seen, and recorded as an assumption for you to confirm later.
- **Recommendation:** A, or C if that is quicker — either ends the wait. Not B: a documented guess
  has the same cost as A and buys a delay rather than a saving.

Two things you do **not** need to decide, because they are already settled: who paid and who shared
come from the command line, not from the file (your answer to Q-001), and re-importing the same
file warns and requires `--again` (your answer to Q-003).
