## Context

**WI-0004, round 2.** Nothing here is for the stakeholder; this is a document that the answers to
`Q-001`, `Q-002` and `Q-003` have made untrue, and `refine` does not write `docs/`.

`docs/product/vision.md` v7, in its section "Open at the time of writing", says of this item:

> *"What 'a line break' means beyond the `<br>` they named, whether one such cell changes its
> column or only itself, and whether the header row is included, are not settled and are
> `refine`'s to put to them — this document does not guess at them."*

All three were put to the stakeholder and answered on 2026-08-29, and the answers are in AC1, AC3,
AC6 and AC7 of `item.md`. The sentence is now false in the one place a reader goes to find out
what is open, and `vision.md` is the document the epic's sign-off is read against.

The item is **not** blocked on this: it is Ready without it, and this question is
`blocking: false` so that it does not hold the pipeline. It is filed rather than left to the
close because a false claim in `docs/` is exactly what BUG-0001 was, and because `review-close`
finding it at D7 or D12 costs a rejection instead of an edit.

## Question

**Should `docs/product/vision.md` be updated now to record that the three questions are answered
and what they settled, or left for `review-close` to handle at the epic's close under D7 and
D12?**

## Options considered

- **A — update it now.** `answer-questions` amends "Open at the time of writing" to say the three
  are answered, states what they settled in the stakeholder's own words, and bumps the version with
  a change-log row per `spec/doc-header.md` §3. Consequence: the document is true from now on, at
  the cost of one more version bump before the item that changes the behaviour has even been
  planned. The behaviour is still not built, so the section still says one behaviour is wanted and
  not built — only its "not settled" clause changes.
- **B — leave it for the close.** `review-close` checks D12 on every claim in `docs/` about the
  behaviour the item touched, and would catch it. Consequence: nothing extra happens now, and the
  document is untrue for as long as WI-0004 takes to build — during which `plan`, `implement`
  and `verify` all read it. The precedent is against this: BUG-0001 was filed because a false
  sentence sat in two documents through a whole item.
- **C — update it now and say nothing else.** As A but without the change-log row. Consequence:
  not available; `spec/doc-header.md` §3 requires the bump and the row, and a silent edit to a
  versioned document is the thing that rule exists to prevent.
- **Recommendation: A.** The edit is small, it is confined to one section, and the alternative is
  knowingly leaving a false sentence in the document three later skills read.

## Answer

**Option A — updated now.** `docs/product/vision.md` is at version 8.

The route is `SKILL.md` step 3.2, an answer from intent already recorded: the stakeholder's three
replies are the record, and the only thing in question was whether the document should be brought
into line with them now or at the epic's close. Nothing here is a design decision, so no ADR: an
ADR records a choice that constrains how the software is built, and this constrains nothing.

Three things settled it against option B. The precedent is the strongest: BUG-0001 exists because
a false sentence sat in two documents through a whole item and was re-quoted rather than
re-checked, which is the failure `spec/doc-header.md` §4a was written for. The exposure is real
rather than theoretical: `plan`, `implement` and `verify` all read `vision.md`, and all three run
on this item before `review-close` would have caught it at D7 or D12. And the cost of A is one
version bump on a document that had to gain a row for this change anyway.

What the new section says, and what it does not: it records the four answers in the stakeholder's
own words with the criteria that carry them, and it keeps saying the behaviour is wanted and not
built, because it is. What changed is only that nothing about *what it should do* is waiting on
the stakeholder any more.
