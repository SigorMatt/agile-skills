## Question

delivered:

- **WI-0001 — Align table columns in a stdin-to-stdout markdown filter.** **Delivered.** The
  filter itself: it finds the pipe tables, pads every column to a common width measured by how
  much room the characters take up on screen, and copies everything else through byte for byte,
  silently — including anything it does not recognise as a table.
- **WI-0002 — Honour the alignment markers in a table's delimiter row.** **Delivered.** `:---`,
  `---:` and `:---:` put the text where they say, in every column including the first and the

- **WI-0003 — Recognise a table mdtab laid out with a right-aligned first column.** **Delivered.**
  The fault you refused to have worked around — *"if the tool then can't recognise a table it laid
  out itself, that's a fault in the tool and I'd want it sorted"* — is fixed.

  last sign-off and said the notes being wrong was our business to fix.
- **WI-0004 — Leave a cell containing a line break plain, whatever its column marker says.**
