## Question

Do all cards live in one flat pool, or does the user organise them into named decks that can be
reviewed separately?

## Options considered

- **A — one flat pool.** Every card is just a card; a review covers everything due. Consequence:
  the smallest thing that works, and it matches your sentence literally. If you study two
  unrelated subjects, they interleave in the same session and you cannot review only one.
- **B — named decks.** Cards belong to a deck; you can review one deck or all of them.
  Consequence: what you want if this holds more than one subject. Costs a deck argument on
  adding, a deck concept in storage, deck selection at review, and a decision about what happens
  to cards added without naming one — which is a fourth question I have not asked because it only
  exists if you answer B.
- **C — free-form tags** instead of decks. Consequence: more flexible than B and more work than
  both; a card can be in several groupings, and "review due cards" needs a filter language to be
  useful. Hard to call simple.
- **Recommendation: A**, on the grounds that you described one activity and one pile. Answer B if
  you already know you will put more than one subject in this — that is the case where A becomes
  annoying within a week, and it is much cheaper to decide now than after WI-0001 ships.

## Answer

[human] One flat pool. It's just vocab, one pile is fine.
