## Question

When reviewing a card, what does the user record — just right or wrong, or a finer grade?

## Options considered

- **A — right or wrong.** Two keys. Consequence: fastest possible review, nothing to think about
  beyond the card itself, and it is all a fixed ladder (`Q-002` option A) can use. A card you
  got right after a long struggle is recorded identically to one you knew instantly.
- **B — a four-way grade** (again / hard / good / easy). Consequence: what SM-2 needs to work,
  and it lets the schedule respond to difficulty. Costs the user a judgement call on every single
  card, which is the most common complaint people have about tools that ask for it.
- **Recommendation: A**, and it pairs with `Q-002` option A. If you answer B to `Q-002`, this
  should be B as well — SM-2 driven by a binary answer is SM-2 with most of its input thrown
  away. The two answers that make sense together are A+A and B+B; if you want to mix them, say so
  explicitly and I will record it as your decision rather than assume it was a slip.

## Answer

[human] Just right or wrong. I don't want to sit there grading myself on a four-point scale for every card.
