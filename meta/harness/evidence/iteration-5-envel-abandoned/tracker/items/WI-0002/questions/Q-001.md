## Context

WI-0002, refinement round 1, question 1 of 3. These three arrived together and are one
conversation about the item that gives you income, spending and balances; they are split into
separate files only so that each answer has its own place in the record.

You have `envel new` and `envel list` already, and `envel list` prints one line per envelope with
nothing on it but the name — that is what was built and checked for WI-0001. This item adds the
money: `envel income groceries 400`, `envel spend groceries 60`, and the arithmetic that says
`groceries` now holds 340.

The thing nobody has said is **where you look to see that 340**. Your original sentence was "record
spending against an envelope, and see what's left in each", which says you want to see it and does
not say where. Two shapes are possible and they are genuinely different, because one of them
changes something you already have:

- `envel list` grows a second column, so the command you already type shows the name and what is
  left in it. Every envelope, one line each, name and number.
- `envel list` stays exactly as it is — names only — and a second command shows the balances.

This is not a naming question. If `envel list` gains a column then WI-0001's delivered behaviour
changes: two of its acceptance criteria are about what a line of `envel list` contains, and they
were verified against lines that contain a name and nothing else. Changing that is entirely
allowed — it is your tool — but it is a change to something already signed off, and it should be
you who asks for it rather than us who quietly do it.

## Question

When you want to see what is left in each envelope, do you want the existing `envel list` to show
the balances, or do you want `envel list` to stay as it is and a different command to show them?

## Options considered

- **A — `envel list` shows the balances.** One line per envelope, the name and the number.
  Consequence: one command, and the one you already know. What is left in every envelope is the
  first thing you see, which is the question the tool exists to answer. Cost: WI-0001's AC2 and
  AC4 are about a `list` line whose only content is the name; both would be amended, and this
  item would carry a criterion recording that they were deliberately changed rather than broken.
  There is no way back to a names-only listing except by asking for one.
- **B — `envel list` is unchanged, and a second command shows balances** — `envel balances`, or
  whatever it ends up called. Consequence: nothing already delivered changes, and the two views
  stay separate: one for "what are my envelopes called", one for "what is in them". Cost: two
  commands where you may only ever want one, and the everyday question needs the less obvious
  command.
- **C — `envel list` shows balances, and a flag turns them off** — the names-only output stays
  reachable. Consequence: both views, one command. Cost: an option to remember, and WI-0001's two
  criteria still change, because the default changes.
- **Recommendation (ours, not yours):** **A**. Everything you told us points at the balance being
  the thing you want in front of you — "see what's left in each" was in your first sentence, and
  "the number is the truth" was your reason for refusing an overspend. A names-only listing is
  what the tool could show before it knew about money, rather than something you asked for. We are
  putting it to you anyway because it un-does a piece of finished work, and that is your call.

## Answer

[human] A — `envel list` shows the balances. The number is the whole reason I open the tool;
I'm not going to type a second command to see it. Change the criteria on the earlier item, that's
fine by me — I'm asking for it. I don't need a flag to hide the balances.
