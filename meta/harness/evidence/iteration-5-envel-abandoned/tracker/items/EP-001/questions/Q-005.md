The four questions before this one come from what *we* need in order to build the thing: how
income gets in, what happens when you overspend, how a transaction gets a month. That list is
made of our gaps, not yours, and it has a blind spot exactly the size of everything you would
have thought to mention unprompted — how the output should read, what the amounts look like,
what would annoy you, what you would want on the first day and what you would never use.

So this question exists to make room for that, and it is filed as a real artifact so the people
who refine, build and review this work can read your answer even though they were not in this
conversation. It does not block anything: work carries on whether or not you answer it.

## Question

What else matters to you about this tool that we have not asked you about?

## Options considered

This one is deliberately not a choice between options. Offering options would be us filling the
gap again with our own ideas, which is the exact thing it exists to avoid. Answer it in whatever
words you like, or say "nothing" — that is a real answer and it will be recorded as one.

## Answer

[human] Three things. I need to be able to move money from one envelope to another — that's how I
cover an overspend. I make mistakes weekly in the spreadsheet, so I need to correct or remove a
spend I've already recorded; I care about that far more than about keeping a history of what I
got wrong. And whatever is left in an envelope at the end of the month stays in that envelope —
it doesn't get swept anywhere — but the monthly summary should show me what each envelope took in
and what went out of it.
