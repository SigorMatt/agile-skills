## Context

EP-001, intake round 1, question 2 of 5 — the same conversation as Q-001, about the tool you
asked for.

You said you "put income into" the envelopes. That sentence has two quite different shapes behind
it, and which one you mean decides what the tool has to know about your money. In the first, the
tool only ever knows about envelopes: your pay arrives, you decide there and then that £400 goes
to groceries, and you tell the tool that. It has no idea what your pay was or whether you
allocated all of it. In the second, the tool knows the whole amount that came in and how much of
it you have parcelled out, so it can also tell you what is still sitting unallocated.

This affects WI-0002 directly (`tracker/items/WI-0002/item.md`) and it changes what the monthly
summary in WI-0003 can show you.

## Question

When income arrives, do you want to record it straight into named envelopes, or record the whole
amount first and then allocate it out, so the tool can also show you what is not yet allocated?

## Options considered

- **A — envelopes only.** Income is recorded directly against a named envelope. The tool never
  knows your total income and never shows an unallocated figure. Consequence: fewer commands and
  nothing to reconcile; if you forget to allocate part of your pay, the tool cannot tell you,
  because it never knew about it.
- **B — a pool, then allocation.** Income is recorded once as a lump, and a second step moves
  money from that pool into envelopes. The tool shows an "unallocated" figure alongside the
  envelope balances. Consequence: it catches money you have not assigned, and the summary can
  show it; it costs a second command every payday, and it introduces a state where your money is
  in the tool but in no envelope.
- **Recommendation:** we lean to A, because your sentence "put income into them" reads as putting
  money into envelopes rather than into the tool, and because A is the smaller thing to build and
  B can be added later without invalidating it. But B is the one that catches a real mistake, and
  if you want that we would rather build it now than bolt it on.

## Answer

[human] A. Money comes in a few times a month and I decide the split there and then, envelope by
envelope. I don't want the tool holding a pool or working out splits for me — I tried that in the
spreadsheet and hated it.
