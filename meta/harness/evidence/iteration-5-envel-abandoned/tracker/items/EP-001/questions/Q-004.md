## Context

EP-001, intake round 1, question 4 of 5 — the same conversation as Q-001, about the tool you
asked for.

You want "a simple monthly summary I can look at". For a summary to be about a month, every
transaction has to belong to a month, and nothing you said fixes how it gets one. If the tool
stamps each entry with the moment you typed it, then a shop you did on the 31st and record on the
1st lands in the wrong month, and there is no way to fix it — this tool does not edit past
entries (`tracker/items/EP-001/item.md`, `## Out of scope`). If instead you can give a date when
you record, the summary matches what really happened, at the cost of another thing to type.

This decides whether the recording commands in WI-0002 take a date at all, and it is what AC4 of
WI-0003 turns on (`tracker/items/WI-0003/item.md`).

## Question

Should a transaction's date be whenever you happen to record it, or should you be able to give
the date it actually happened?

## Options considered

- **A — the date you record it, always.** The tool reads the clock and that is the date.
  Consequence: nothing extra to type, and no way to be wrong about the format; anything you enter
  late is filed under the wrong month for ever, and catching up after a week away puts a week of
  shopping into one day.
- **B — an optional date, defaulting to today.** You normally type nothing and get today; when
  you are catching up you give the real date. Consequence: the common case stays as short as A,
  late entries land correctly, and the summary is trustworthy; it adds one option to specify and
  a date format to agree on.
- **Recommendation:** we lean to B. The whole value of a monthly summary is that the months are
  right, and A quietly breaks that the first time you record something a day late. A is genuinely
  simpler, though, and if you know you always record as you go, it is one less thing.

## Answer

[human] B. Normally I'll be typing it the same day and want to type nothing extra, but I do catch
up after a few days and those need to land on the day they happened.
