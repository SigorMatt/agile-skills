## Context

EP-001, intake round 1, question 3 of 5 — the same conversation as Q-001, about the tool you
asked for.

Envelope budgeting exists to make overspending visible, so what the tool does at the moment you
overspend is a decision about the point of the tool rather than a detail. You record a £60 shop
against "groceries", and "groceries" has £45 left. Nothing in what you told us says what should
happen next, and the three plausible answers give you three different tools.

This is the difference between an acceptance criterion that says "records the spend and exits 0"
and one that says "refuses and exits non-zero", so WI-0002 cannot be finished without it
(`tracker/items/WI-0002/item.md`).

## Question

What should the tool do when you record a spend larger than what is left in that envelope?

## Options considered

- **A — record it; the envelope goes negative.** The balance shows as −£15 and the summary marks
  it. Consequence: the tool never stops you doing what you actually did, and your records match
  reality; a negative balance is a thing you then have to notice and act on.
- **B — refuse it.** The tool prints an error, records nothing, exits non-zero, and you have to
  move money into the envelope first. Consequence: envelope balances can never lie, and the
  discipline is enforced; but a spend that really happened is missing from your records until you
  do the paperwork, which is the moment people stop using a budgeting tool.
- **C — record it and warn.** It goes through, the envelope goes negative, and the tool says
  loudly on stderr that it has. Consequence: A's honesty with a nudge; slightly more to specify,
  since "warn" has to be defined well enough to test.
- **Recommendation:** we lean to C — record it, let it go negative, and say so on stderr with a
  zero exit — because the record staying true to what you spent seems more valuable than the tool
  refusing. If you want the discipline instead, B is a perfectly coherent tool and we will build
  that.

## Answer

[human] B — refuse it and tell me. I don't want an envelope going negative; the whole point is
that the number is the truth. If the money has to come from somewhere else, I'll move it between
envelopes myself and then record the spend.
