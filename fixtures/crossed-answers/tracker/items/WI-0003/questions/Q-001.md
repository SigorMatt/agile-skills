## Context

## Question

Should a cell with a line break in it obey its column's marker?

## Options considered

- **A —** yes, like every other cell.
- **B —** no, it sits top-left.
- **Recommendation:** none, insufficient basis.

## Answer

A cell with a line break sits top-left, plain, whatever the column marker says.
